1. What anni.run is

anni.run is a free editor for the CSS animations and values in your own projects. Your coding agent opens files from your project in a session on anni.run; you tune them on a timeline and the agent writes the result back. There are no accounts: whoever opens a session holds its owner token.

2. Your content

  • Everything you or your agent upload stays yours. You allow anni.run only what it needs to run your session: store the files, process them and show them to you and to the people you let in.
  • Only upload what you are allowed to use and share. Don't upload passwords, keys, other secrets or personal data of others. For code like that, use local mode, where nothing leaves your machine.
  • You decide who sees a session. Anyone with its link can ask for access; a public session can be watched by anyone who has the link. You are responsible for what you share and with whom.

3. Storage and deletion

  • Each session is deleted 7 days after its last activity, or right away when its owner closes it. The shared example session on the homepage is the only one that stays.
  • There are no backups. Keep your own copies – your project files stay on your machine and are the original.
  • Uploads are limited to 1.5 MB per file and 24 MB per session.
